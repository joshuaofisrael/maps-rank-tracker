Why did my Google Business Profile ranking drop?

By the RankFootprint team · Last updated 26 October 2026

Short answer: drops usually come from a change on your profile, a change on a competitor's, or a change at Google. Before you react, confirm the drop is real across your area, not just from one search on one phone.

Step 1: confirm it's real

Local results differ by searcher location (Google Business Profile Help: Tips to improve your local ranking), and a single check can mislead you. Compare the same keyword over the same grid of points before and after. A real drop shows up across many points, not just one.

Step 2: check your own profile

  • Recent edits: changing the name, category or address can trigger a review and shift rankings (Google Business Profile Help: Edit your Business Profile).
  • Suspension or warning: look for notices in your profile (Google Business Profile Help: Fix suspended or disabled profiles).
  • Category changes: a new or removed primary category can change which searches you're relevant for.
  • Reviews: a run of removed or negative reviews can affect prominence (Google Business Profile Help: Tips to improve your local ranking).

Step 3: check competitors

A new competitor nearby, or one that moved closer, can push you down without you changing anything. Look at who now ranks above you at the points where you dropped.

Step 4: check Google

Google posts confirmed ranking updates and incidents on the Google Search Status Dashboard. If a drop lines up with a broad update, the fix is usually steady improvement rather than a single change.

Step 5: fix the cause, then re-measure

Reverse any accidental edits, fix guideline issues (Google Business Profile Help: Guidelines for representing your business), and keep earning real reviews. Re-scan the same grid a few weeks later so you compare like with like.

FAQ

Can editing my Google Business Profile hurt my ranking?

It can. Edits to key fields like the name, category or address may go into review and change which searches you show up for. Make edits deliberately and only when they're accurate.

How do I know if Google had an update?

Check the Google Search Status Dashboard, which lists confirmed ranking updates and incidents.

Should I create a new profile if my ranking dropped?

No. Duplicate profiles break Google's guidelines and can lead to a suspension.
