Google Business Profile categories and Maps rank

By the RankFootprint team · Last updated 5 November 2026

Google Business Profile categories tell Maps what you do. The primary category is the strongest relevance signal for many local searches. Secondary categories cover extra services. Getting them wrong is a common reason a grid looks strong for one phrase and empty for another.

What Google says about categories

Google's Business Profile help asks owners to choose the category that best describes the business, then add secondary categories only for services you actually offer. Categories should match the storefront and website. Fake or stacked categories can lead to suspension or ignored signals.

How categories show up on a Footprint map

After you change a primary category, run the same keyword grid you used before. Look for coverage gains on tiles that previously showed 11+ or not found. If only the office pin improved, the category change may have helped relevance without fixing distance or prominence gaps. See what grid rank tracking is and how to read a Maps rank grid.

Practical checklist

  • Pick one honest primary category competitors in your niche also use for the searches you care about.
  • Add secondary categories for real services, not wish list keywords.
  • Keep the website service pages aligned with those categories.
  • Rescan on a schedule so you can tell whether the edit moved tiles. How often to check rankings.

Categories are one lever. Reviews, distance, and on-page relevance still matter. Google describes local ranking as relevance, distance, and prominence.

Frequently asked questions

What is a primary Google Business Profile category?

It is the main category on your profile that best describes the business. Google uses it heavily for relevance on local searches.

How many secondary categories should I add?

Only categories for services you truly offer. Extra unrelated categories rarely help and can hurt trust.

How soon will a category change move my map pack rank?

There is no promised timeline. Re-measure with the same grid after a few weeks so you see the trend, not one lucky search from your desk.

Do categories replace needing a website?

No. An accurate site that matches the profile still helps customers and helps Google understand the business.

Can RankFootprint change my Google categories?

No. We measure where you appear across a grid. You edit categories inside Google Business Profile.
