Rules of thumb


Area typeTypical radiusSuggested gridSpacing


Dense city centre1 mi5×50.5 mi


Suburb / town3 mi7×71 mi


County-wide trade10-15 mi9×92.5-3.75 mi


These are our own suggestions, not fixed rules. If points are much more than a mile apart in a busy city, you can miss changes between them. If the grid goes far beyond where you work, it adds "not found" points that don't matter.
