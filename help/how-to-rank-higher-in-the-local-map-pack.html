How to rank higher in the Google local map pack

By the RankFootprint team · Last updated 26 October 2026

Short answer: Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence (Google Business Profile Help: Tips to improve your local ranking). You can't change distance. You can improve relevance (an accurate, complete profile in the right category) and prominence (reviews, links and mentions). Then measure across your area rather than from one spot.

What Google confirms

  • Relevance: complete, accurate details, the right categories, services and hours.
  • Distance: how far you are from the searcher or the location in the search.
  • Prominence: how well known you are, including review count and rating and your presence across the web.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: Tips to improve your local ranking.

A priority list that respects the rules

  1. Get the primary category right. Add only secondary categories that are true.
  2. Use your real business name. Adding keywords to the name breaks Google's guidelines and risks a suspension (Google Business Profile Help: Guidelines for representing your business).
  3. Fill in services, hours, attributes, photos and a plain description (Google Business Profile Help: Edit your Business Profile).
  4. Earn reviews steadily from real customers, and reply to them (Google Business Profile Help: Tips to get more reviews).
  5. Make your website's location pages match the profile, and add structured business details (Google Search Central: Add business details to Google).
  6. Earn local links and mentions: suppliers, local news, associations. Practitioner surveys like the Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors survey rank links and reviews highly. Treat that as informed opinion, not Google policy.

Why "rank #1" is the wrong goal

Because distance is a factor, nobody ranks first everywhere. A better goal is to grow the share of your service area where you're in the top 3, and to know which neighbourhoods are weak so you can focus there.

What not to do

Don't use fake addresses, keyword-stuffed names, bought reviews or multiple profiles for one location. All of them break Google's guidelines (Google Business Profile Help: Guidelines for representing your business) and can get the profile suspended (Google Business Profile Help: Fix suspended or disabled profiles).

FAQ

What is the local map pack?

It's the block of usually three business listings with a map that Google shows for local searches, above or among the regular results.

How long does it take to rank in the map pack?

It varies a lot with competition and how much work is needed. Nobody can honestly promise a timeline. Measure monthly so you see the trend.

Do I need a website to rank in the map pack?

No, but an accurate website that matches your profile helps Google understand your business, and it gives customers somewhere to go.
