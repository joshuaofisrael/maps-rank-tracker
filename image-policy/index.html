Image policy

By the RankFootprint team

This page explains how images are used on RankFootprint.

Most pages use diagrams, the RankFootprint logo, and simple graphics that illustrate a Maps rank grid. When a page includes a photograph or an illustrative image, the caption says so and links back to this policy.

We do not use a photo to invent a client result. The logo and social preview are original graphics.

Questions about an image can go through the Contact page.
