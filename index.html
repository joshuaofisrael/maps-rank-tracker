How it works

1. Drop your pin

Pick your business and a search phrase customers actually use, like “emergency plumber”.

2. Lay the grid

We place a grid of check points (3×3 up to 9×9) across the radius you serve.

3. Read your footprint map

Each tile shows your position from that spot, coloured from strong to missing.

What you get

Footprint maps

A colour grid of your rank across your area, saved each time you scan.

Next-step tips

Plain-language suggestions on what to work on next, based on your profile and results.

Client-ready reports

Shareable summaries for agencies and owners, branded your way on higher plans.

Where the data comes from

RankFootprint gets ranking results from licensed third-party search data providers, which return the local results shown for a search at each grid point. RankFootprint does not run its own scrapers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
