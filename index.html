How it works

1. Drop your pin

Pick your business and a search phrase customers actually use, like “emergency plumber”.

2. Lay the tiles

We place a grid of check points (3×3 up to 9×9) across the radius you serve.

3. Read the TileMap

Each tile shows your position from that spot, coloured from strong to missing.
