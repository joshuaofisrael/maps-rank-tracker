Questions

What is a Google Maps grid rank tracker?

It checks where a business appears in Google Maps results from a grid of points across its service area, instead of one location, and shows the rank at each point on a map.

How many points does a scan check?

A 3×3 scan checks 9 points, 5×5 checks 25, 7×7 checks 49 and 9×9 checks 81. The free scan is 3×3.

Where does RankFootprint's ranking data come from?

From licensed third-party search data providers that return the local results shown for a search at each grid point. RankFootprint does not run its own scrapers and is not affiliated with Google.

How much does RankFootprint cost?

Plans are $19, $29 and $49 a month, billed monthly by Stripe and cancellable any time. A free 3×3 scan is available without a plan.
