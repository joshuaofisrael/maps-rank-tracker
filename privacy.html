Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, operates RankFootprint and respects your privacy.

What we collect

Information you submit (name, business, location, search phrase, email) and basic usage data.

How we use it

To run scans, reply to you, and improve the service. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

With service providers who help us run RankFootprint: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (hosting, data storage), SerpApi (search data: your business name, location and search phrase are sent to fetch results), Stripe (payments and billing; we never see full card numbers) and our email provider (scan results and sign-in links). We share data otherwise only where law requires.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and measure site performance. It is privacy-focused: it sets no cookies, does not use local storage, does not fingerprint you or track you across sites, and does not collect personal information such as your IP address for tracking. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Cookies

If you sign in, we set one essential session cookie (rf_session) for up to 30 days. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

Shared results

Each scan has a private, hard-to-guess link. Anyone you give the link to can view that scan.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your data through our contact form.

Governing law

This policy is governed by Florida law.
