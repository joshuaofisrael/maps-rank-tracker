Founding plans: early access

RankFootprint is in early access, so we're opening 4 founding spots: 2 Starter, 1 Growth and 1 Agency. These are the founding limits, and we'll raise them as we add scan capacity. During early access, each scan is run and delivered within 24 hours of your request rather than instantly. Your price stays the same for as long as you stay subscribed. If we can't deliver a scan, just tell us and we'll refund that month.

Where the data comes from: Scan results come from SerpApi, a third-party API that retrieves Google Maps search results on our behalf. RankFootprint is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or SerpApi. Results reflect what Google showed at the time of the scan and may differ from what any individual user sees. We do not guarantee any ranking. No plan guarantees any traffic or sales result.
